# **Session 10 Reflection**

**Date:** 17 October 2026\
**Topic:** AI Scalability, Customer Promise, Cost Optimization, Operational Ownership & Observability

This session helped me understand that scalability is not just about handling more users but about maintaining the value promised to customers as the product grows.

The Uber example highlighted that technical availability does not always mean customer satisfaction. A product is successful only when it delivers the expected outcome. I also learned that as customers become more dependent on a product, reliability and downtime become much more critical.

Another important takeaway was AI cost optimization. Using the most powerful model for every request may not be economical. Smaller models can handle simpler tasks, while complex tasks can be routed to advanced models. This creates a balance between cost, latency and output quality.

Finally, observability and traceability are essential for AI products. Monitoring only application code is not enough; models, prompts, datasets and APIs must also be tracked to identify changes in product behaviour.

**My key learning:** Scalability means preserving customer value while managing growing usage, costs, dependencies and operational risks.
