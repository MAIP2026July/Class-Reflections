A child in an apartment who has only met Gurkha security guards will tell you that security guards are Gurkhas. The child is not bad. That is all the child has seen. The model is the same. Limited data, confident inference, and nobody inside the system knows it is incomplete.

Our training material is status reports written by the people being judged on those reports. That is not a sample of delivery health. It is a sample of what engagement managers are willing to type. If a practice has a culture of writing green and burying risk in polite sentences, Helm will learn that culture and call it a pattern. We will then rank against it. The bias is not a racist label in a table. It is the only world the model has been allowed to see. Session 6 already said consultants did not consent to this second use. This session named the mechanism. Exposure, not intent.

**Collect less.** Data is a drug, and teams will ask for more of it. Age, name, gender, even the author's identity on a status report, all of it can leak a signal we do not need. Academic papers go out without the author's name so the reviewer cannot play favourites. If we can rank an engagement without scoring the person who wrote the report, we should. Our ethics note already says we assess engagements, not people. The data model still has the author's name sitting next to the text, which means we have not actually taken the stand. I will argue to drop it from the extraction schema, and to accept that some ranking quality may fall. Foregoing data is a product decision, not a privacy slogan.

**Motherhood statements.** Fairness, privacy, interpretability, only in the context of this product. For us, fairness is not a hiring test. It is whether a quiet account is as likely to be flagged as a noisy one, and whether a well written green report is not rewarded for being well written. If I cannot say that in a sentence a delivery director would recognise, I have not done the work.

**Incident communication.** If a bank says 3,000 customers were affected and I do not know whether I am one of them, the fear is the incident. We need the version of that message before we need the breach. If you have not heard from us, your account was not in the set. I would rather write that paragraph now than invent it under panic.
