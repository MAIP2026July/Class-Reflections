# Reflection: Session 12

31 October 2026 | Gangrade Pranjal Jitendra | 2025MB26023

---

The presentation is not a tour of how we built Helm. It is ten minutes to convince someone to put money in. Sell the value, not the story of the build. If the CEO is travelling, the CTO still has to close.

I have been writing the deck as a walk through of the prototype. That is the habit of a delivery lead showing a steering committee that the work happened. A buyer does not care that we have six Apex actions. They care whether quiet trouble stops being skipped. Session 9 already said the promise is not a ranked list. This session said the same thing as a time limit. If I cannot say the investment case in ten minutes, I do not have one.

**Words that will start a fight later.** Accurate, user friendly, beautiful, easy. The faculty's point was contractual, not literary. If you leave those words in the spec, your vendor will build their version of accurate, and you will spend the project arguing after the contract is signed. I went through our PRD the same evening. We still have phrases like "high confidence" and "timely" without a number next to them. High compared to what. Timely against an eleven day slip or against a weekly cycle. Session 4 already had the actionable window. We wrote the idea and then let the word timely back in as if the window did not exist.

The test he gave was to feed the document to an AI and ask whether it can build this, and what it needs clarified first. That is a better review than another teammate reading for tone. If the model asks what "unsafe output" means, a human builder will too.

**What they are actually buying.** People do not buy insurance. They buy a safety net. The hospital example made this sharp. Doctors paid to keep people healthy, not to run more tests, so the organisation's money and the patient's interest finally point the same way. Our buyer is not purchasing a briefing. The RDM is buying the right to spend the week on the few engagements that matter, without discovering in month three that a green report was red inside. If I pitch features, I am selling insurance. If I pitch that, I am selling the safety net.

**Where I push back.** Target cost, work backwards from what the customer will pay, is right and I still do not have that number. Session 7 told me to read the customer's accounts. I agreed. I have not done it. Putting a rupee on one avoided escalation is the homework this session repeated, and agreeing with it twice is not the same as doing it.
