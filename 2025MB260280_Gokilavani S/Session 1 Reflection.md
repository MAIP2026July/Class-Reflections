Session 1 Reflection

Date: 25 July 2026

Topic: Introduction to Product Management and AI


I walked into Session 1 with a fairly simple picture of a Product Manager. In my mind, a PM was the person who keeps teams connected, tracks timelines and makes sure the work gets finished. By the end of the class, that picture felt incomplete. Coordinating the work is only one part of the job. The harder part is making sure the team is solving the right problem in the first place.
What stayed with me most was the idea that a PM is responsible for the outcome without having direct authority over the people who deliver it. Engineering, budget and technical choices often sit outside the PM's control. I found this both challenging and interesting. It means a PM cannot simply give orders. They have to earn trust, explain the purpose clearly and bring people along. They also have to make decisions when the information is incomplete, which takes real confidence. I could relate this to project management, where I have seen that a well-coordinated project still does not guarantee a solution that creates value.
The database example made this very clear to me. A technically impressive option is not always the right product decision. We also have to ask whether the team can maintain it, what it will cost and whether it actually improves the customer's experience. It reminded me that the best technology and the best choice are not always the same thing.
The part that changed my thinking most was the discussion on AI. We looked at three ways AI connects with product management: using AI to build products, using AI in the PM's own daily work, and making AI a part of the product itself. Until now, I mostly saw AI as a feature offered to customers. Now I see it can support the whole journey, from the first idea to the final product.
I leave this session more curious than before. I realised that Product Management is less about managing tasks and more about judgement, communication and responsibility, and I am looking forward to learning more in the coming sessions.
