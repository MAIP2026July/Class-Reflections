## What the session covered

- **Defining AI Products:** What makes AI a product capability, not just a feature
- **Core AI Product Types:** Prediction, recommendation and generation in practice
- **Why AI Products Emerged:** Data, cheap compute, better models, new distribution
- **Indian Market Context:** Scale, multilingual demand and digital public infrastructure
- **Consumer, Business, Regulation:** Who gains, who bears risk, who asks for control
- **Foundations for Later Sessions:** Which product decision is AI actually improving?

## Key ideas and cases

### Ideas that stayed with me

- **AI must earn its place:** AI can be the product, an invisible optimizer or a decorative add-on. Only the first two justify the investment.
- **Archetype drives discipline:** Predicting, recommending and generating need very different evaluation, UX and risk handling, even on the same model.
- **India changes the design brief:** UPI-scale volumes, many languages and price sensitivity make trust and low cost design constraints, not afterthoughts.

### Cases that made it concrete

- **Google Maps traffic:** AI as the decision layer: value shows up as fewer wrong turns, not as an 'AI feature'.
- **GitHub Copilot:** AI embedded in the workflow; success is measured by tasks completed, not by novelty.
- **UPI and DPI:** Public rails made high-frequency, low-ticket AI use cases commercially viable.
- **EU AI Act:** Regulation decides who must explain a decision and who carries the risk when it is wrong.

## How my thinking shifted

| Before this session | After this session |
| --- | --- |
| 'AI-powered' is a positioning label | 'AI-powered' is a claim that needs proof in user outcomes |
| Start from model capability | Start from the decision the product needs to improve |
| Regulation is a later compliance step | Regulation shapes the product from the first concept |

## Applying it to my work

- **Audit our AI claims:** List every 'AI' feature in our payment products and tag it as core value, optimizer or decoration.
- **Frame features as decisions:** Rewrite each AI idea as the merchant or customer decision it improves, with a measurable target.
- **Design for Bharat scale:** Treat language, low-end devices and trust signals as day-one requirements for merchant-facing AI.

## Key takeaway

> Before any AI build starts, ask whether the workflow becomes better, faster, safer or more defensible. If not, it is ornament.
