## What the session covered

- **Finding AI Opportunities:** Where AI creates leverage worth paying for
- **Feasibility and Value:** Feasibility, upside and risk in one frame
- **Build, Buy, or API:** What is commodity and what is worth owning
- **Platform Versus Feature:** Wedge feature, reusable capability or platform
- **Jobs-to-be-Done for AI:** Customer progress before technology choices
- **Strategy Artifacts:** Memos and scorecards leadership can debate

## Key ideas and cases

### Ideas that stayed with me

- **Valuable vs merely easy:** An opportunity can look attractive because it is easy to build. Only value and feasibility together justify the bet.
- **Own only what differentiates:** Use APIs for commodity capability and build where proprietary data or workflow creates advantage.
- **Strategy is sequencing:** Not every attractive AI idea deserves the next unit of attention. Good strategy is selective and unromantic about hype.

### Cases that made it concrete

- **Fraud scoring in fintech:** High value and feasible: strong signals, clear cost of error, measurable loss reduction.
- **OpenAI API integration:** Fast to market, but differentiation must come from data and workflow, not the model.
- **Canva AI features:** AI as a feature strengthening an existing ecosystem rather than a standalone platform.
- **Stripe for merchants (JTBD):** Merchants hire fraud prevention to reduce anxiety and lost revenue, not to 'use AI'.

## How my thinking shifted

| Before this session | After this session |
| --- | --- |
| More AI opportunities means a stronger roadmap | A strong roadmap rejects most fashionable AI ideas |
| Build in-house to stay in control | Build only where ownership creates strategic advantage |
| Start from technology capability | Start from the progress the customer is trying to make |

## Applying it to my work

- **Score our AI backlog:** Rate each idea on value, feasibility and risk, then kill or park the bottom half.
- **Build/buy/API per capability:** Separate commodity pieces (OCR, LLM) from what we must own (merchant risk signals).
- **Write a one-page opportunity memo:** Frame the next AI bet so leadership can fund, debate or reject it on evidence.

## Key takeaway

> Good AI strategy feels selective, evidence-backed and unromantic about hype.
