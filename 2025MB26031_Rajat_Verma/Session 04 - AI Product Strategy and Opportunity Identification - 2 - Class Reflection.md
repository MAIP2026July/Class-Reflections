## What the session covered

- **Product-Market Fit for AI:** Value, trust and repeat use
- **ChatGPT as a Platform:** Why some AI products escape the feature trap
- **Amazon-Style Recommendations:** Discovery, basket lift and habit formation
- **Competitive Positioning:** Data advantage, workflow fit, switching costs
- **Opportunity Mapping Activity:** Choosing the right company opportunity first
- **Strategy Synthesis:** Turning AI strategy into a roadmap

## Key ideas and cases

### Ideas that stayed with me

- **Curiosity is not PMF:** AI products attract trial easily. PMF shows in repeat use, trust and economics, not sign-ups.
- **Defensibility comes from workflow:** Models commoditise; data loops, workflow embedding and switching costs make a product hard to replace.
- **Strategy must change the org:** A real AI strategy reshapes roadmap, team structure and capability investment, not just the feature list.

### Cases that made it concrete

- **ChatGPT adoption:** Massive trial; the platform story depends on ecosystem and repeat work use.
- **GitHub Copilot retention:** Retention comes from being inside the daily developer workflow.
- **Spotify / Amazon recs:** Recommenders win through discovery and habit, not just conversion.
- **Perplexity vs search:** A challenger needs a clear workflow edge to overcome incumbent distribution.

## How my thinking shifted

| Before this session | After this session |
| --- | --- |
| High usage proves product-market fit | Repeat use and trust after novelty fades prove PMF |
| The best model wins | The best workflow fit and data loop win |
| Pursue every promising AI bet in parallel | Protect focus: pick the bet that builds credibility first |

## Applying it to my work

- **Track retention, not trial:** Measure 30/60/90-day repeat use for AI features in our merchant apps.
- **Identify our data moat:** Find which transaction and merchant signals competitors cannot easily replicate.
- **Internal copilot first?:** Weigh an internal ops copilot (lower risk) against a customer-facing AI feature.

## Key takeaway

> PMF for AI products must be argued through behaviour, trust and economics, not feature novelty.
