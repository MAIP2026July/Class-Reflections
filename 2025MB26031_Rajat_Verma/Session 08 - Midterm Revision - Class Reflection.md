## What the revision covered

- **Read the Product First:** Classify the product pattern before the technology
- **The AI PM Under Pressure:** Ownership, influence and escalation in one frame
- **Choose the Bet:** One strong recommendation beats many ideas
- **Catch the Expensive Mistake:** Design, data and operations fail together

## Key ideas and cases

### Ideas that stayed with me

- **Classify before analysing:** Naming the product pattern first (predict, recommend, generate) makes the rest of the answer sharper.
- **Recommend, don't list:** A clear choice with stated trade-offs is stronger than a complete list of frameworks.
- **Failures are connected:** Design, data and lifecycle mistakes usually appear together, so good answers connect them.

## How my thinking shifted

| Before this session | After this session |

| Long answers show knowledge | Short, decision-led answers show mastery |
| Topics are separate chapters | Topics are one connected product story |

## Applying it to my work

- **Recommendation-first updates:** Open status updates and reviews with the decision, then the trade-off, then the evidence.
- **Classify incoming AI asks:** Tag each new AI request by product pattern before discussing tools or vendors.
- **Pre-mortem across layers:** Before a launch, check design, data and operations risks together.

## Key takeaway

> The midterm is a compressed product review: classify well, choose clearly and name the trade-offs.
