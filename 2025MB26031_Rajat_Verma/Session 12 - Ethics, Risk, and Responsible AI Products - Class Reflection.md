## What the session covered

- **Bias in AI Products:** Where harm enters before the model acts
- **Privacy Concerns:** Necessary, excessive or indefensible data
- **Hallucinations:** Recourse and responsibility under uncertainty
- **Responsible AI Design:** Which risks deserve a pause or tighter rollout
- **Governance Mechanisms:** Making responsible AI operational
- **Risk Assessment Activity:** Protecting users without freezing progress

## Key ideas and cases

### Ideas that stayed with me

- **Ethics must change what ships:** Governance is real only when it changes what the team ships, pauses, documents or escalates.
- **Minimum data for value:** Ask what data is needed for value and what is merely tempting.
- **Score risk, don't just debate it:** Likelihood, severity and detectability together decide whether to block launch or constrain rollout.

### Cases that made it concrete

- **Apple Card limits:** Opaque credit decisions became a public fairness crisis.
- **Lawyer citation failures:** Hallucinated output caused real professional harm.
- **Support bots inventing policy:** The company owns what its bot says to customers.
- **EU AI Act governance:** Risk-tiered rules turn ethics into operational requirements.

## How my thinking shifted

| Before this session | After this session |
| --- | --- |
| Responsible AI is a values statement | Responsible AI is a set of operational thresholds |
| Risk review slows delivery | Clear launch gates let teams move faster with confidence |
| Hallucination is a model flaw | Hallucination is a product responsibility |

## Applying it to my work

- **Risk memo for credit features:** Map harm scenarios in merchant lending to mitigation, escalation path and owner.
- **Bias checks across segments:** Test approval rates by region, merchant size and category before launch.
- **Grounded support bot:** Restrict the bot to approved policy sources and escalate when unsure.

## Key takeaway

> Governance becomes real only when it changes what the team ships, pauses, documents or escalates.
