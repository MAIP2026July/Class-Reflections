# Session 1 Reflection

**Date:** 25 July 2026  
**Topic:** Course Intro, PM Role & The 3 Pillars of AI  

The biggest mindset shift from today’s session was moving from seeing a Product Manager as just a coordinator to someone who actually owns the problem and the outcome. Coming from a software engineering background, it’s easy to treat PM work as just managing schedules and specs, but the PM is ultimately accountable for whether the product actually solves the problem after it goes live.

**Key takeaways & thoughts:**

* **Ownership vs. Authority:** In reality, a PM rarely has direct control over budgets, tech stacks, or engineering bandwidth. Ownership isn't about control; it's about making good decisions with limited info and influencing teams without direct authority.
* **Tech choice $\neq$ Product choice:** The example about choosing a database hit home. A cutting-edge database might be technically superior, but if the team can't maintain it easily, it’s a bad product decision. The same applies to AI—the most advanced model isn't always the right business choice if latency or cost ruins the user experience.
* **The 3 AI Pillars:**
  1. *Using AI to build products* (dev tools, pipelines)
  2. *Using AI for PM workflows* (synthesis, research)
  3. *Embedding AI into the product* (features)

I used to focus only on the third point, so seeing how AI fits into PM work and engineering workflows opened up a broader view.
