Introduction
The class on Management of AI Products helped me understand the importance of managing artificial intelligence as a business product rather than focusing only on the technology behind it. Developing a successful AI product requires a clear understanding of customer needs, business objectives, available data, and the value that the product can deliver.

Key Learnings
One of the important takeaways from this subject is that AI products should be developed to solve real-world problems. An AI solution becomes valuable when it addresses a specific user need and produces meaningful business outcomes.
I also understood the importance of identifying the target audience, defining the product's objectives, and evaluating whether AI is the right approach to solving a particular problem. Product managers need to consider both technical feasibility and business value before moving forward with development.

Business Value of AI Products
AI can help organisations improve efficiency, automate repetitive tasks, analyse large amounts of data, and support better decision-making. However, implementing AI alone does not guarantee success. The product must be useful, reliable, and aligned with the organisation's goals.

Challenges in Managing AI Products
Managing AI products can involve challenges such as data quality, development costs, privacy, bias, and user trust. These challenges highlight the importance of responsible AI practices, continuous evaluation, and collaboration between technical teams and business stakeholders.

Personal Reflection
This class helped me recognise that successful AI product management requires a combination of technological knowledge, business understanding, analytical thinking, and customer-focused planning. As a management student, I can apply these concepts when evaluating business problems and identifying opportunities where AI may create value.

Conclusion
Overall, this class strengthened my understanding of the relationship between artificial intelligence, product management, and business strategy. It encouraged me to think about AI not simply as a technology, but as a tool for creating practical solutions and sustainable business value.
