Introduction
The class on Management of AI Products helped me understand the role of artificial intelligence in developing products and solving business problems. AI product management involves identifying customer needs, setting clear objectives, and ensuring that AI-based solutions provide practical value to users and organisations.

Key Learnings
One of the key lessons I gained is that an AI product should begin with a clearly defined problem. Before developing a solution, product managers need to understand the target users, their requirements, and the expected outcomes.
I also learned that AI product development requires collaboration between business teams, technical experts, and other stakeholders. A successful product must balance technical possibilities with customer expectations and organisational goals.

Application of AI in Business
Artificial intelligence can help businesses automate routine activities, analyse data, improve customer experiences, and support decision-making. However, organisations must carefully evaluate the costs, benefits, and limitations of AI before implementing it.

Challenges and Responsible AI
AI product managers need to consider data quality, privacy, fairness, accuracy, and user trust. Responsible management is important to ensure that AI products are reliable, ethical, and beneficial to society.

Personal Reflection
As a management student, I understand that AI is not only a technical concept but also a business opportunity. Learning about AI product management encourages me to think critically about business challenges and explore how technology can provide useful solutions. I also recognise the importance of understanding customer needs and evaluating the real value of an AI product.

Conclusion
Overall, this class helped me appreciate the importance of combining business strategy, customer understanding, and artificial intelligence. I believe these concepts will be useful in analysing business problems and identifying opportunities for innovation in the future.
