Introduction
The class on Management of AI Products helped me develop a broader understanding of how artificial intelligence can be connected with business strategy, product development, and customer needs. Artificial intelligence is becoming an important part of modern businesses, helping organisations improve their operations, make informed decisions, and develop innovative products and services. However, the successful implementation of AI depends not only on the technology but also on how effectively it is planned, developed, managed, and evaluated.
This class encouraged me to think about AI from a management perspective and understand how organisations can use it to solve real-world problems and create business value.

Key Learnings
One of my main learnings is that successful AI product development should begin with identifying a clear problem. Before developing an AI-based product, it is important to understand the needs of customers, the challenges they face, and the outcomes they expect. A product manager must determine whether artificial intelligence is an appropriate solution and whether it can provide better results than existing methods.
I also understood the importance of having clear product objectives. These objectives help organisations decide what they want to achieve, identify the resources required, and evaluate the success of the product. Without proper planning, even a technically advanced AI product may fail to meet customer expectations or deliver meaningful business results.
Another important learning is that AI product management requires collaboration between different teams. Business managers, product managers, data scientists, software developers, designers, and other stakeholders may need to work together. Each team contributes different knowledge and skills, making communication and coordination essential throughout the product development process.

AI Product Development and Business Strategy
AI products should be aligned with the overall goals of an organisation. Businesses need to evaluate how an AI solution can improve efficiency, reduce costs, enhance customer satisfaction, or create new opportunities. Product managers must also consider the feasibility of the proposed solution, including data availability, technical requirements, financial investment, and expected benefits.
I learned that product development does not end when a product is launched. AI products need continuous monitoring, testing, improvement, and evaluation. Their performance may change as customer needs, business conditions, or available data change. Regular feedback and performance assessment can help organisations identify problems and make necessary improvements.
This made me understand that AI product management is an ongoing process that requires both strategic planning and flexibility.

Business Applications of Artificial Intelligence
Artificial intelligence has applications across various industries. Businesses can use AI to analyse customer behaviour, identify patterns in data, automate repetitive tasks, improve recommendations, and support decision-making. These applications can help organisations work more efficiently and provide better services.
However, adopting AI should not be viewed as a goal in itself. Organisations must identify the actual business problem and evaluate whether the proposed technology offers sufficient value. They should also consider implementation costs, employee training, integration with existing systems, and the willingness of customers and employees to adopt the new solution.
This helped me recognise the importance of balancing innovation with practical business requirements.

Challenges and Responsible AI Management
Another important aspect of AI product management is understanding its limitations and potential risks. AI systems depend heavily on the quality of the data used to develop and operate them. Inaccurate, incomplete, or biased data can lead to unreliable results and poor decisions.
Privacy and data security are also important considerations, particularly when AI products process personal or confidential information. Organisations must use data responsibly and establish appropriate safeguards. In addition, AI-generated results should be evaluated carefully because AI systems may sometimes produce inaccurate or misleading information.
These challenges highlight the responsibility of product managers to consider fairness, transparency, reliability, accountability, and user trust. A successful AI product should not only be innovative but also safe, useful, and responsibly managed.

Personal Reflection and Application
As an MBA student, I found the subject relevant because it connects technology with business management and strategic decision-making. It helped me understand that managers do not necessarily need to develop AI systems themselves, but they should understand their capabilities, limitations, costs, and potential business applications.
I can apply this understanding when analysing business problems, evaluating new technologies, and identifying opportunities for innovation. For example, before recommending an AI solution for an organisation, I would first identify the problem, understand the requirements of the users, assess the availability of suitable data, and consider the expected benefits and risks.
I also realised that customer satisfaction should remain an important consideration throughout the product lifecycle. Technology creates value when it addresses genuine needs and provides a positive user experience. Therefore, effective AI product management requires analytical thinking, communication, planning, and continuous learning.

Conclusion
Overall, this class strengthened my understanding of the relationship between artificial intelligence, product management, and business strategy. I learned the importance of identifying the right problem, setting clear objectives, collaborating with stakeholders, evaluating business value, and considering ethical responsibilities.
The class encouraged me to look beyond the technical aspects of AI and think about how it can be applied meaningfully in organisations. I believe that these concepts will help me develop a more practical and strategic approach to understanding emerging technologies and their role in business innovation.
