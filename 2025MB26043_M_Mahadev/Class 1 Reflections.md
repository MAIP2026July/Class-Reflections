# Class 1 Reflections

25 July 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- A product manager has to think like the CEO of the product: own the outcome, not just the backlog.
- AI is not a product strategy by itself. It is a capability that only matters if it solves a real customer problem.
- Frameworks are useful, but they support judgment; they do not replace it.

**What stayed with me**
The idea that a PM without enough technical understanding becomes a "postman", just passing messages between business and engineering without adding value. To make good trade-offs you need to understand what is easy, what is hard, and why.

**What I will take forward**
Before getting excited about an AI feature, I will first ask: what problem does this solve, and for whom?
