# Class 11 Reflections

24 October 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Ethics, risk, responsibility and governance in AI products.
- Bias is often unintentional and comes from historical data, labelling and society; it must be actively checked.
- There is no universal fairness test; fairness, privacy and safety must be defined for the specific product and its users.
- Collect less data: data minimisation reduces risk.
- Human review is not free, and no launch is completely bug-free.

**What stayed with me**
We cannot outsource accountability to AI. Ethics should not stay as "motherhood statements"; they need to become concrete, measurable actions.

**What I will take forward**
Plan for continuous monitoring, audits and clear incident communication after launch.
