# Class 2 Reflections

01 August 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- The product lifecycle and launch readiness: a beta is only halfway, not the finish line.
- Evidence and product intuition both matter; good PMs know when to rely on which.
- Some decisions are better deferred until there is enough information. Deciding too early can be costly.
- Rapid prototyping and experiments are a cheap way to learn before committing real money.

**What stayed with me**
The point that a PM is often spending someone else's money. That changes how carefully you should prioritise and how honestly you should report progress.

**What I will take forward**
Keep a simple trail of why decisions were made (artifacts and traceability), so the reasoning is clear later, not just the outcome.
