# Class 3 Reflections

18 August 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Building a product is different from solving a customer problem. Customers pay for progress in their own work, not for features.
- Product vs project: a project ends, a product keeps evolving.
- With AI, the target keeps moving, so the plan has to adapt.
- Transformation and optimisation are different goals and need different expectations.

**What stayed with me**
The discussion on value capture and pricing: if the product creates value, the company should capture a fair share of it, and that should be anchored to the customer's benefit, not to our cost.

**What I will take forward**
When describing any product idea, I will start from the customer's progress ("what can they now do better?") instead of listing features.
