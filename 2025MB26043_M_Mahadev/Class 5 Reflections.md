# Class 5 Reflections

29 August 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Build vs buy vs partner: you do not need to own the model, but you should own the judgment layer that makes the product yours.
- Product-market fit has to be measured, not assumed.
- Design for uncertainty and trust, because AI outputs are not always right.
- Human-in-the-loop has to be designed properly; it is not just a checkbox.
- Cost is more than tokens: testing, integration and support also cost money.

**What stayed with me**
The materiality filter: not every AI error matters equally, so focus effort on the errors that actually hurt the customer or the business.

**What I will take forward**
Experiment small before scaling, and be honest about which parts of the solution we really need to build ourselves.
