# Class 6 Reflections

05 September 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Start with the problem, not the technology.
- AI is most useful when we reimagine the workflow, not just add AI on top of the old one.
- Customer feedback has hidden meaning; people often do not say exactly what they feel (implicit vs explicit sentiment).
- Data comes with responsibility: consent, governance and accountability.

**What stayed with me**
The articulation gap: customers often cannot clearly explain what they need. The PM has to observe and understand the real context instead of only taking requests literally.

The surveillance paradox was interesting: the more data we collect to understand customers, the more they may feel watched. So consent and transparency are part of the product design, not just a legal step. Since this was the mid-point of the course, it was also a good moment to connect the earlier sessions together.

**What I will take forward**
Measure meaningful outcomes rather than "beautiful" metrics that look good but do not reflect real customer value.
