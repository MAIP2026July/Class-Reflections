# Class 7 Reflections

12 September 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Value-based pricing: price according to the value created for the customer, not the cost to build.
- The value shows up on the customer's balance sheet, so pricing should move beyond "hours saved".
- Willingness to pay belongs to people (buyers and users), not to organisations in general.
- The AI product lifecycle has extra challenges: probabilistic outputs, trust, and dependency on outside model providers.

**What stayed with me**
A real PM leads from the front and takes ownership of the whole product experience, including the parts they do not directly control.

**What I will take forward**
Set clear boundaries on what the AI should and should not do, and keep building trust after launch, not just before it.
