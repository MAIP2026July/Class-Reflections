# Class 8 Reflections

03 October 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Reliability matters more than an impressive demo.
- A prototype can answer the wrong question; make sure it tests what actually matters.
- A product should "earn the right" to pass each stage gate before moving forward.
- Automate gradually and keep accountability with people.
- Swapping one model for another is not effortless; the design has to allow for change.

**What stayed with me**
The gap between a demo that works once and a product that works every day for real users.

**What I will take forward**
Keep governance practical and lightweight, and choose the technology based on the problem rather than what is popular.
