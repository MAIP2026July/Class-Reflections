# Class 9 Reflections

10 October 2026 | M Mahadev | 2025MB26043

**Key learnings**
- Business value matters more than technical accuracy. A model metric should not replace the business metric.
- Averages hide the failures that hurt the most; look at edge cases, not just average performance.
- Performance has to be monitored after launch, not only tested before.
- Products are promises to customers, not feature catalogues.

**What stayed with me**
The question of who pays for the model's errors. Often it is the customer, not the company, and that should change how carefully we design.

**What I will take forward**
Plan for the "unknown unknowns" and balance profitability with responsibility.
