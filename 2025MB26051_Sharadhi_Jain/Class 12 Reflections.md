* AI product will be trained with bias and there could be bias

* Policy bias can be there. Like in paternity and maternity leave based bias
* Do not collect unnecessary data than essential.
* Decision must be made on what events truly worth capturing.
* Minimum data that we collect is the better, have a consent of data and declare the purpose of collecting the data
