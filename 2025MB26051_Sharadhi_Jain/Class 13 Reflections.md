* I learned that product management is not only about developing a product but also about understanding customer needs and business goals.

* Clear requirements are important for building a product that meets customer expectations.

* A product should be planned with a long-term vision rather than focusing only on immediate requirements.

* Setting measurable goals helps track product performance and understand whether the product is successful.

* Workflow design helps us understand how different users and teams interact with a product.

* Human involvement is important in AI-based products, especially when decisions require review or approval.

* AI systems should have proper fallback options to handle situations where they cannot provide accurate results.

* AI tools can improve productivity, but their outputs should always be reviewed and validated.

* Proper documentation helps the development team understand product requirements, workflows, and system design.

* C4 architecture diagrams help explain the structure of a system at different levels.

* A product roadmap helps organize future features and development activities based on business priorities.

* A successful product should provide clear value to its customers and solve their actual problems.

* Monetization is important because a product should have a clear plan for generating revenue.

* Target costing helps businesses plan product costs while keeping customer expectations and profitability in mind.

* AI products involve additional costs such as API usage, tokens, infrastructure, and model inference.

* Different pricing models, such as subscription-based, usage-based, and hybrid pricing, can be used depending on the product.

* While developing AI products, we need to balance cost, response time, accuracy, and reliability.

* Customer retention is important, and businesses should understand why customers continue using a product or decide to downgrade.

* Understanding unit economics helps determine whether a product can remain profitable as the number of customers increases.

* A good product presentation should clearly explain the problem, proposed solution, customer benefits, and business value.
