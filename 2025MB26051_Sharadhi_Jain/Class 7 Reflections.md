Summary of the reflections and key points from Session 7 :

**Product Management Mindset**
 
 **Lead from the Front**: Product managers should not act as detached executives operating out of a corner office. Successful PMs understand the product deeply, engage directly with operational details, and should even experience customer support firsthand (e.g., handling tickets, emails, or calls). 
 
 **Contextual Problem Solving**: In the case discussion regarding an electricity distribution company (DISCOM) customer support system, solutions must address real operational realities (such as handling planned vs. unplanned outages, managing peak call volumes, and predicting faults proactively). 

 **Pricing & Value Creation**
 **Value-Based Pricing**: Pricing should not be arbitrary; it should be rooted in the tangible and intangible value delivered to the customer (e.g., cost savings from reduced power wastage, optimized workforce, or improved public sentiment/reputation).   
 
 **Market Dynamics**: When selling B2B software to regulated entities like DISCOMs, factors such as government regulations, regional differences (urban vs. rural margins), and leadership motivations must be factored in.  
 
 **Value Share**: Suppliers generally capture a fraction (typically targeted around 20%) of the total economic value or savings generated for the customer.  


**AI Product Lifecycles & Challenges**

**Probabilistic Nature & Trust**: Unlike traditional software, AI-based products exhibit probabilistic behavior. UI/UX design must clearly communicate confidence levels (e.g., distinguishing between confirmed states vs. uncertain inferences).   

**Supply Chain & Dependencies**: AI products rely heavily on external vendors, APIs, and foundational models. PMs must account for third-party risks, subscription costs, and potential model deprecations.   

**Proactive Interventions**: Transitioning from reactive customer support to proactive intelligence (such as automated outage detection via smart grids, location-based GPS routing, and push notifications) creates significantly higher value.
