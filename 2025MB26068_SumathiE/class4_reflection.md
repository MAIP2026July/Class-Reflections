# Class 4 Reflection

## Session 4: Opportunity Identification -- 2

### 1. From AI Capability to Business Value

The session described the value-realisation path as:

**AI capability → User value → Adoption → Business value**

An AI capability creates business value only when it delivers meaningful
value to users and is adopted.

### 2. Product-Market Fit (PMF)

Product-Market Fit (PMF) means that the product solves a sufficiently
important customer problem.

To assess PMF for an AI product, both types of metrics are needed:

-   **Model metrics**
-   **Product metrics**

Model performance alone is not enough; the product must also solve a
meaningful customer problem and deliver value in use.

### 3. Platform Network Concept

The platform network concept was illustrated as a reinforcing cycle:

**More capabilities → More users / use cases → More usage → More
feedback → Product improvement**

As the platform gains capabilities, it can attract more users and use
cases. Increased usage generates more feedback, which can help improve
the product further.

### 4. Competitive Positioning

The notes identify several ways an AI product can strengthen its
competitive position:

-   Better data
-   Better model / application
-   Better user experience (UX)
-   Workflow integration
-   Distribution
-   Trust
-   Cost
-   Speed

## Session 4: Strategy Synthesis

### 5. Eight Questions for AI Product Strategy

A strategy can be synthesised by answering these eight questions:

1.  **WHO:** Who is the target user?
2.  **WHAT:** What problem are we solving?
3.  **WHY AI:** Why is AI better than the current solution?
4.  **HOW:** What product / model approach will we use?
5.  **VALUE:** What is the business / user value?
6.  **DIFFERENTIATION:** Why us?
7.  **RISK:** What could go wrong?
8.  **MEASUREMENT:** How do we know it works?

These questions help connect the target user and problem to the AI
approach, value, differentiation, risks, and measures of success.

### Reflection

An AI product opportunity is not valuable simply because the technology
works. It must solve an important customer problem, deliver user value,
and achieve adoption to create business value. Product-Market Fit
requires looking at both model and product metrics. A sustainable
strategy should also explain who the product serves, why AI is
appropriate, how it will be delivered, why it is differentiated, what
risks exist, and how success will be measured.
