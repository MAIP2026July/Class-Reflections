# Class 7 Reflections

## 1. AI Product Life Cycle

Discover → Develop → Validate → Deploy → Monitor → Improve

An AI product follows a continuous improvement loop.

### AI Product Feedback Loop

Problem → Data → Model → Evaluate → Deploy → Monitor

The monitoring stage generates feedback, which is used to retrain or improve the model. The process then repeats.

## 2. Reliability and Safety Concepts

- **Circuit breaker:** Automatically stops or rolls back an AI system when predefined thresholds are breached.
- **Guardrail:** Prevents unacceptable harm while pursuing the KPI.
- **KPI (Key Performance Indicator):** Measures success.

## 3. Experimentation and Deployment Strategies

- **Shadow testing:** Uses real inputs to assess the impact of a new decision or model without replacing the live decision.
- **Canary:** Exposes a small portion of live traffic or users to a new version.
- **A/B testing:** Compares a control group and a treatment group under controlled allocation.
- **Multi-armed bandit:** Dynamically allocates traffic between alternatives based on the observed reward.
