## 1. Key Learnings & Takeaways

- The biggest learning for me was that simply calling something an “AI product” does not make it an AI product. I need to understand whether AI is actually creating the value, acting as an invisible optimizer, or has simply been added as a feature.
- I found the distinction between traditional rule-based systems and learning systems useful because AI products can behave differently as the underlying data and environment change.
- The three AI product categories — prediction, recommendation and generative AI — gave me a useful way to classify the type of problem a product is actually solving.
- I also liked the three different ways AI appears in product work: AI used to build the product, AI used by the PM for product work and AI embedded inside the product.

## 2. What Resonated Most With Me

What stayed with me most was the idea that the PM ultimately owns the product outcome even though the PM may not personally build the technology. That changes how I look at the role. I should not think of myself as someone who simply passes requirements between business and engineering. I need to understand why the product exists, what value it is supposed to create and whether the AI capability is actually contributing to that value.

I also found the “AI as a source of value vs AI as decoration” distinction very practical. It is easy for teams to add AI because it sounds innovative. The harder question is whether removing the AI would materially change the customer outcome.

## 3. Constructive Thoughts & What Could Be Explored Further

I would like to explore more examples where the same product contains both traditional software and AI components. Understanding where AI should deliberately NOT be used could be just as important as knowing where to use it.

## 4. Personal Action Item

For any AI-enabled feature I come across in my work, I want to ask one simple question first: **“What customer outcome becomes better because of AI?”** If I cannot answer that clearly, I should question whether AI is really needed.
