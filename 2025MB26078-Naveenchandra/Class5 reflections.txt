Class5 reflections, Aug 29

1.The "Double Bearing" Trap: Calling a product "AI-powered" without delivering measurable user value is marketing fluff
Customers won't pay for empty buzzwords; they pay when operational friction is eliminated and repeatable workflows actually improve

2.The Four-Layer AI Stack: Product architectures must be separated into Data, Model, API, and UX layers
Standardizing through APIs prevents vendor lock-in, ensuring the underlying model remains a pluggable utility rather than a rigid dependency

3.UX for Probabilistic Systems: Generic legal disclaimers fail users
The UX layer must communicate uncertainty, display confidence thresholds, and provide visible evidence trails so customers don't place blind trust in hallucinatory outputs

4.Strategic Opportunity Scoring: Strategy is not about adopting every trendy capability; it is about having the discipline to reject weak ideas
Opportunities must be strictly scored on user pain, frequency, feasibility, and time-boxed urgency

5.Internal Supply Chain vs. P&L Products: Internal multi-vendor software represents a procurement supply chain challenge
Only products packaged and sold directly to external markets carry true P&L accountability, demanding unit economics and margin scrutiny

6.The Human-in-the-Loop Fatigue: Human oversight is an operational bottleneck, not an effortless safety net
When reviewers face aggressive operational time pressure, fatigue causes rubber-stamping, completely eroding trust and system governance

7.Midterm Case-Based Reality: Midterm evaluations test executive decision-making under uncertainty, not textbook recall
Strict one-page constraints per sub-question penalize unstructured text dumping and demand concise, high-conviction recommendations

8.Managing the Management: When human reviewers behave erratically, it stems from how leadership measures them
Product managers must proactively negotiate realistic evaluation KPIs with executive leadership rather than measuring superficial task volumes
