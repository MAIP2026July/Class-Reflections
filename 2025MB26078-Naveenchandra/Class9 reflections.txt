Class9 reflections, Oct10


1.Managing Rather Than Coding: The AI PM's job is not building models from scratch
Our responsibility is orchestrating the product, validating that the underlying AI hypothesis removes real customer friction, and defending business outcomes

2.The Economics of the Confusion Matrix: Mathematical accuracy is dangerously misleading in business
In lending, a 90% accurate model that misclassifies even a few bad loans still destroys profitability, because one default wipes out 40 profitable loans

3.Who Bears the Cost of Errors?: PMs must map who pays for false positives versus false negatives
In payment fraud, banks only prioritized model investments once legal frameworks shifted financial liability away from consumers directly onto institutions

4.Moving Beyond Mathematical Metrics: Precision and recall hide operational truths if separated from business context
PMs must pair offline statistical scores with real-world KPIs: operational review load, customer complaint rates, and net margin impact

5.A/B Testing Against Shifting Baselines: Testing AI products on third-party cloud LLMs lacks a stable baseline
Model providers continuously alter inference compute and run background experiments, meaning external platform changes can silently contaminate your internal product A/B tests

6.Tail Risks Over Averages: High aggregate accuracy masks catastrophic edge cases
A camera AI confusing a strapped guitar with a missing helmet highlights how systems fail unexpectedly in real environments, demanding specialized edge-case testing over aggregate benchmarks

7.Evolving Measurement with Product Maturity: A metric that serves an early-stage launch becomes obsolete as the product scales
PMs must systematically revise evaluation criteria across lifecycle stages, or they risk optimizing outdated targets while missing critical customer churn

8.Frugal and Governed Feedback Loops: Not all customer feedback loops should be automated into model retraining
PMs must actively choose which feedback signals to reinforce and which to discard, preventing erratic customer behavior from causing model over-fitting

9.Accountability for the Core Promise: Features, marketing campaigns, and slick demos are merely forward-looking promises
A product's true market viability and reputation depend entirely on whether users reliably realize actual economic value during everyday, post-launch operations
