# Session 10 Reflection

**Date: 17 October 2026**

**Topic: AI Scalability, Customer Promise, Cost Optimization, Operational Ownership & Observability**

This session made me realise that scaling an AI product is not simply about supporting more users or adding more infrastructure. As the number of users increases, factors like cost, latency, availability and external dependencies can directly affect the customer experience. The product still needs to deliver the same value even as usage grows.

The discussion around the **customer promise** was particularly interesting. Customers are more concerned about whether they receive the expected outcome than whether the system is technically running. The Uber example made this easy to understand. Even if the app is working perfectly, if a customer cannot actually get a ride, the product has not fulfilled its purpose. This made me think about reliability more from the customer's perspective.

Another useful takeaway was around **AI cost optimization**. Using the most powerful AI model for every request may not always make business sense. Simple tasks could be handled by smaller or less expensive models, while more capable models can be used for complex tasks. So model selection is not only a technical decision but also a balance between quality, speed and cost.

I also understood that scalability goes beyond infrastructure. As a product expands to more customers, locations or use cases, we can face issues with data quality, model performance, APIs and changing user behaviour. External dependencies can also become limitations if third\-party services have capacity or rate limits.

The discussion on **observability and monitoring** also stood out to me. Monitoring should not only tell us whether the system is running but also whether the customer experience or business outcome is being affected. Having proper versioning and traceability also becomes important in AI because changes in models, prompts or data can affect product behaviour.

Overall, my biggest learning from this session was that **scalability means maintaining the customer promise as the product grows**. A successful AI product should be able to balance reliability, cost, performance and customer expectations as adoption increases.
