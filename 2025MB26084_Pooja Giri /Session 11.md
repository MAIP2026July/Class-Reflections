# Session 11 Reflection

**Date: 24 October 2026**

**Topic: Responsible AI, Bias, Privacy, Hallucination, Accountability & Risk\-Based Launch Decisions**

This session made me realise that **Responsible AI needs to be considered from the beginning of product development**, rather than something we check only before launching. An AI product may perform well technically, but we also need to think about whether its decisions are fair, safe and transparent for the people using it.

The discussion on **bias** was particularly useful. AI models learn from the data available to them, and if that data contains existing biases, the model can also produce biased outcomes. This becomes especially important in areas like recruitment, lending or identity verification where AI decisions can directly affect people.

Another important learning was around **privacy and data minimization**. Just because we have access to certain customer data does not mean we should automatically collect or use it. We should understand why the data is required, how it will be used and whether collecting it creates unnecessary risk.

The discussion on **hallucination** also stood out to me. Sometimes the right behaviour for an AI system is to say that it does not know rather than confidently provide an incorrect answer. Having options such as retrieving additional information, escalating to a human or providing a fallback can make the product more trustworthy.

I also understood that simply having a human involved does not automatically make an AI system responsible. There should be clear accountability around who reviews important decisions, how users can question an outcome and who ultimately owns the decision when something goes wrong.

Overall, my biggest learning from this session was that **Responsible AI is about thinking through the consequences when the system gets something wrong**. As a PM, we need to balance business value and model performance with fairness, privacy, transparency and risk before deciding how widely an AI product should be launched.
