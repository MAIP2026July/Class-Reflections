# Reflection: Session 7

**12 September 2026 | Jodhdeep Singh | 2025MB26112**

### The story that landed

* **Data privacy is a core product responsibility.** Under laws like **DPDP (India) and GDPR (Europe)**, organizations are trustees of user data. Weak consent and security can leave a product "dead on arrival".
* **Surface-level sentiment is not enough.** Positive/negative/neutral labels hide what customers actually feel about specific parts of the product.
* **Aspect-based sentiment analysis.** Extracting the target, feature, sentiment, opinion holder and context gives feedback a PM can act on.
* **Consent, minimization and purpose limitation** should shape what data is collected and why, from the start.

### Where I disagree

Aspect-based analysis is powerful, but it depends on enough high-quality text feedback. For products with sparse or purely technical feedback, the extra tooling may cost more than it returns.

### What I will do

* Include **privacy and consent requirements in the PRD** rather than adding them late.
* Partner with security and legal teams on **data governance by design**.
* Move beyond a single satisfaction score and tag feedback **by feature and aspect**.

### Open questions

1. What are good ways to capture aspect-level sentiment when feedback is mostly logs or tickets rather than reviews?
2. How should consent be handled when a new use for existing data emerges later?
