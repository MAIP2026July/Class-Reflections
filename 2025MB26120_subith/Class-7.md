### Session 7 – Class Observations (12.09.2026)

- **Product ownership:** PMs remain accountable for the overall product outcome, even when multiple teams, vendors and external partners are involved.

- **Understanding users:** Direct interaction with users often reveals practical problems that reports and dashboards may not capture. I could relate this to my healthcare leadership experience.

- **Problem-solving:** The electricity distribution case highlighted the importance of understanding underlying processes rather than addressing only visible symptoms.

- **PFIR framework:** Separating facts/features, inferences and recommendations helps structure analysis and prevents assumptions from being presented as facts.

- **Proactive customer experience:** AI can help identify problems before customers report them, but uncertain information must be communicated responsibly.

- **Value-based pricing:** Product pricing should reflect the economic value delivered to customers, not just the cost of developing and operating the product.

- **Post-launch responsibility:** AI products require continuous monitoring, fallback mechanisms and vendor management to address model changes, data drift and operational risks.

**Key takeaway:** Effective product management requires end-to-end ownership, direct customer understanding, measurable value creation and continuous responsibility beyond product launch.
