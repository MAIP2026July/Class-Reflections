### Session 8 – Class Observations

- **Managing AI unpredictability:** AI outputs can vary even with the same instructions, creating risks in production. Deterministic rules are preferable when consistent results are essential.

- **Learn before automating:** Human checkpoints help identify exceptions and understand a process before moving towards full automation.

- **Staged implementation:** Prototypes, pilots and scaling should follow clear evaluation criteria before progressing to the next stage.

- **Managing vendor dependency:** A flexible, pluggable architecture allows components to be replaced when providers change pricing, models or availability, without rebuilding the entire product.

- **The PM’s role in decision-making:** AI can generate suggestions quickly, but the Product Manager must validate assumptions, assess risks and ensure that speed does not compromise quality.

**Key takeaway:** Successful AI product management requires balancing automation with human judgement, flexibility with simplicity, and speed with responsible decision-making.
