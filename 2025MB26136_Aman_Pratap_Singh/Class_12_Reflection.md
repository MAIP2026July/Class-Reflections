**Class 12 Reflection**

Management of AI Products | 31 October 2026 | Project feedback and monetisation

The first half was live feedback on Aishwarya's team's diabetes app. Several points apply to everyone. Use markdown files so AI can review them. Put team details in a README. Give real numbers instead of words like 'accurate' or 'reduced time'. Define workflows now instead of saying 'later'. He also showed PlantUML and Mermaid for diagrams as code, and swimlanes to show who does what, including where AI and humans sit.

The monetisation part started from target costing, the Japanese approach. Work backwards from what the customer will pay, and don't add a margin on top of your own cost. The biggest idea for me was input versus output pricing. Vendors like input-based pricing such as T&M or per-token API billing because it carries less risk. Customers prefer paying for outcomes. The real estate lead example, paying per sale and not per lead, showed how that can earn more if you can carry the risk.

He also said the pricing should match how the customer experiences value. His example was why he should pay for Claude during a Diwali week when he doesn't use it.

On unit economics, heavy users can wipe out margins, so stress-test the pricing. Sometimes it's better to downsell than to lose a customer. His Oracle licensing story showed how cost can drive the architecture, which is why architects need some MBA thinking too.
