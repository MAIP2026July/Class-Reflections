**Class 13 Reflection**

Management of AI Products | 14 November 2026 | Team project presentations

This session was the first round of project presentations, with feedback that everyone has to log as GitHub issues for their own project too. Six teams presented, including CarePulse, PolicyGuard AI, Helm, safecar, HRLI and Idea Hub AI.

Some feedback came up again and again. Every project needs a swimlane diagram with AI steps and human-in-the-loop marked separately, and has to show where AI uncertainty is shown in the UI. We should cover the four pillars of domain, tech, regulator and consumer behaviour, including what motivates each stakeholder to change. He also wants it clear who pays for the AI tokens.

The biggest learning was to keep two separate calculations: our own startup's profitability for the VC, and the customer's case for buying. Customers expect about three to four times what they pay. He asked PolicyGuard to stress-test the \$150-an-hour analyst cost against cheaper offshore rates, and he asked several teams for numbers beyond year one.

He was firm with HRLI that a product built for one organisation is a project, not a product. He also made Idea Hub's tool the evaluator for all our repos, which is a nice 'eat your own dog food' test. It can be tuned for the course but not for each team.
