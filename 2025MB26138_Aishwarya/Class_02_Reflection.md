**Class 2 Reflection**

1 August 2026 - Product lifecycle, artefacts and frameworks

Today was the lifecycle - discover, define, strategise, plan, build, launch, grow, retire - and the point that it's a loop, not a waterfall, which is obvious once said out loud but I don't think I've actually operated that way.

"Trustees of someone else's money" is the phrase I wrote down. Even on an internal build, it's not my budget or my timeline to burn casually. The push to keep decision records - why we chose X, traceable from architecture through to the user manual - is something I'd actually never bothered doing, mostly because nobody asked.

The house-building analogy (fix the structure early, leave paint for later, but settle big stakeholder mismatches up front) is a decent way to decide which arguments are worth having now versus later. I'm less sold on the long framework list - JTBD, RICE, Kano, MoSCoW, RACI and the rest - feels like a lot to actually reach for mid-sprint, but I get that they're meant as aids, not gospel.
