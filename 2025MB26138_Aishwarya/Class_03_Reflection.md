**Class 3 Reflection**

18 August 2026 - Frameworks in practice, product vs project, Scrum

Small thing that stuck: never say "user" unqualified - name them, farmer, parent, student, whoever - because the unqualified word lets you get away with a vague persona. I catch myself saying "the client" at work the same lazy way, covering institutions that behave nothing alike.

The product-vs-project line was clearer than I expected: build for one customer and it's a project, however good it is. I've built a couple of internal tools that were really just projects wearing a product's clothes, and calling them that out loud changes what I'd do differently.

Scrum as a labour agreement - no changes mid-sprint, everything's up for renegotiation at the boundary - is a framing I hadn't heard before and actually prefer to "the sprint is sacred." The pricing aside, that a vendor usually only captures around a fifth of the value created, is worth remembering next time someone on our side complains a client is paying too little.
