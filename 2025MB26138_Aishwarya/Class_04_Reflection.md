**Class 4 Reflection**

22 August 2026 - When AI actually creates product value

The framing I keep coming back to from today: check social legitimacy before you even get to value or feasibility. Consent, vulnerable groups, no hidden surveillance - that gate comes first, and only then do you ask if AI actually helps. The nuclear-powered pencil sharpener line was funny but it's a real filter.

Rules first, then search/analytics, then predictive ML, and generative or agentic only after that - I'd generally jumped straight to "can an LLM do this" without asking whether a simpler rule would have done it cheaper and more reliably. Going to actually sit with that ordering before our next feature pitch.

The actionable-time-window point (telling a farmer it'll rain a minute before it rains is useless) is obvious in hindsight but it's exactly the kind of thing that gets missed when a demo looks impressive but nobody's checked whether the output arrives in time to matter.
