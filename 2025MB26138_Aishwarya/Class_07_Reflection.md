**Class 7 Reflection**

12 September 2026 - Case thinking with the DISCOM example, pricing, lifecycle

Most of the session was the DISCOM case, and the reframe that mattered was thinking as the product company selling to the utility, not as the utility itself - a distinction I don't think I'd have made cleanly on my own.

The reimagining was the useful bit: detect an outage from consumption pattern before the customer even calls, push planned-maintenance alerts, send a field technician ahead of the complaint. Simple once said, and it reframes support from reactive to something closer to monitoring.

The hallucination risk ladder - truth, facts, inferences, recommendations, risk rising as you go down - is a cleaner mental model than anything I'd had for talking about model output reliability, and I can see using it almost as-is in a conversation with stakeholders who ask "can we trust this."
