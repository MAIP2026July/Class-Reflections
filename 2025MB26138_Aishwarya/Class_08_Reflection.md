**Class 8 Reflection**

3 October 2026 - Prototypes, MVPs, staged automation

The three-banks story was the one that got me: he tells the agent to test with three banks, and it quietly turns that into a permanent rule. I've had almost that exact thing happen with an AI-generated spec that nobody caught until it was half-built - the PM becomes the bottleneck precisely because there are too many proposals to actually read carefully.

Prototype vs MVP vs pilot each earning its own gate, rather than being stages you just pass through, is a distinction I've been sloppy about - calling something an MVP when it's really still answering whether the idea works at all, not whether customers get real value from it.
