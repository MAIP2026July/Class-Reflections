**Class 11 Reflection**

24 October 2026 - Ethics, bias, privacy and responsible AI

The Gurkha example was the clearest explanation of training-data bias I've heard - a child who's only ever seen Gurkha security guards assumes every guard is Gurkha and every Gurkha is a guard, and a model trained the same narrow way does exactly that.

No single fairness framework exists, follow the law and industry guidance as the floor and try to do better - which is less satisfying than wanting a checklist, but probably honest. The point about blind review removing names because names can leak gender is one of those details that's obvious once pointed out and invisible otherwise.

On privacy: collect only what's needed, state the purpose, respect retention limits under DPDP/GDPR, treat children's and health data as a different tier entirely. The product-should-know-when-to-abstain idea (not just when to answer) feels like the right default for anything we'd actually ship.
