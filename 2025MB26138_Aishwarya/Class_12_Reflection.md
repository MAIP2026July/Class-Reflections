**Class 12 Reflection**

31 October 2026 - Project feedback and monetisation

Half the class was live feedback on one team's diabetes app, but most of it generalised: use real numbers instead of words like "accurate" or "reduced time," put team details in a README, define the workflow now instead of saying "later." All fair criticism of habits I probably have too.

The monetisation half was the more interesting part for me - target costing, working backwards from what the customer will pay rather than cost-plus, and the input-vs-output pricing distinction. Vendors like input-based pricing (per-token, T&M) because it's lower risk for them; customers want to pay for the outcome. The real-estate-lead example, paying per sale instead of per lead, made that concrete in a way the theory alone wouldn't have.

His own point about not wanting to pay for an idle subscription during a week he doesn't use it (Diwali) is a small thing but it's basically the customer's side of the same argument - pricing should track how value is actually experienced, not just how it's metered.
