**Class 13 Reflection**

14 November 2026 - Team project presentations

First round of project presentations - CarePulse, PolicyGuard AI, Helm, SafeCar, HRLI and Idea Hub AI all went up, and the feedback repeated across teams more than the content of any one pitch.

The recurring note was to keep two separate calculations apart: the startup's own profitability case for a VC, and the customer's case for buying, where customers generally expect three to four times what they pay back in value. PolicyGuard got pushed hard on whether their \$150-an-hour analyst baseline would survive a cheaper offshore comparison, which is a fair challenge I'd want asked of my own numbers too.

HRLI got told plainly that something built for one organisation is a project, not a product - a line that's going to stick with me given how many "products" I've seen internally that are really one client's project with a nicer name. Turning Idea Hub's own tool into the evaluator for every team's repo was a neat, slightly uncomfortable test of whether it actually generalises.
