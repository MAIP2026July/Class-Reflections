# Class 6 Reflection

**Date:** 5 September 2026  
**Topic:** Workflow redesign, PRDs, data, consent and sentiment analysis

This class made me see why starting with “what can the model do?” can take a product in the wrong direction. A better place to begin is the current workflow. What information enters it, who makes each decision, where time is lost, and what outcome is expected? Only then should we ask where AI can actually improve the process.

I also liked the suggestion to write the PRD in language that the stakeholder would recognize. System language can sound precise while hiding the real need. A frontline worker is not asking for a sophisticated retrieval system. The worker wants a trustworthy answer without stopping the task and searching through several documents.

The sentiment-analysis example showed the weakness of reducing every comment to positive or negative. The useful information is often the subject of the complaint, the feature involved, who expressed it and what expectation was missed. That context is what allows a product manager to act.

For Wayfinder AI, we need two clear workflows: one for people maintaining and approving content, and another for workers asking questions. If we mix them into one general “AI knowledge” flow, we will miss different permissions, delays and success measures.
