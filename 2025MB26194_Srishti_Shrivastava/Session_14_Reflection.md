# Class 14 Reflection

**Date:** 21 November 2026  
**Topic:** Project presentations day 2 and Wayfinder AI

Presenting Wayfinder AI made the course feel more real because we had to explain it as a product and a business, not only as an AI solution. My part on market and competition made me notice how easy it is to place a large TAM on a slide and move on. The harder question is whether the first group of customers has the problem strongly enough to pay for a solution.

The competitor comparison also helped me describe Wayfinder more clearly. Learning systems manage courses, search tools find information, connected-worker platforms support tasks and AI writing tools create drafts. Our proposed gap is the connection between governed content and the questions workers actually ask.

I do not think the model itself can remain our main advantage because other products can access similar technology. The stronger advantage would have to come from the approval workflow, source controls, feedback loop, audit history and the ability to onboard a new site without starting again.

My biggest takeaway is that each claim in the pitch creates something we must prove. A pilot has to show reduced SME effort, useful answers and strong faithfulness to approved sources. The presentation was therefore less of a finish line and more of a list of promises the product must now earn.
