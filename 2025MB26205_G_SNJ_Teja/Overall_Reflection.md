# Overall Reflection

**Course:** Management of AI Products (MBA ZG583), Sessions 1 to 14

Looking back at all fourteen classes, the biggest change in me is how I think about where AI belongs. I started the course thinking about models and features. I'm finishing it thinking about the specific person on the other end, the promise we make to them, the cost of being wrong in rupees, and whether the whole thing is worth paying for. As a QA engineer, I also see my own role differently now. Testing an AI feature isn't about checking that one answer looks right; it's about a fixed set of cases, rerun every time the model or provider changes, with evidence before anything goes live. For AdmitAssist, my next step is clear: let AI fully handle the routine questions, keep the coordinator for the ones that need judgement, and back the price with the school's actual savings.
