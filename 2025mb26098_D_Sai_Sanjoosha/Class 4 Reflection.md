Understanding Where AI Creates Product Value

This class focused on understanding where and when Artificial Intelligence can create meaningful value in a product. One of the most important lessons I learned was that integrating AI into a product does not automatically make it successful or valuable. The actual purpose of using AI should be to solve a customer problem more effectively or improve an existing process.
The faculty discussed examples such as multilingual agricultural advisory solutions, UPI dispute assistants, and Netflix recommendation systems. These examples helped me understand how AI can be applied to different industries and customer requirements. For instance, an agricultural advisory solution should consider factors such as crop stage, location, and weather conditions to provide useful recommendations. This demonstrated that the effectiveness of an AI product depends on how well it understands the user's actual situation.
Another important concept was evaluating the hypothesis behind a product. Before developing an AI-based solution, we should clearly identify what value we expect it to create and how that value can be measured. We should also consider the consequences of incorrect recommendations and identify who could be affected.
The discussion on social legitimacy was particularly valuable. An AI product should be evaluated not only for its technical capabilities but also for ethical considerations, user consent, safety, and its impact on vulnerable groups. We also discussed viability factors such as AI quality, latency, cost, and the availability of resources to support the product.
I found the concept of keeping humans involved in important decisions especially relevant. AI can assist with recommendations and analysis, but human judgment may still be necessary in situations involving significant risks.
My key takeaway: I understood that AI should be introduced only when it provides measurable incremental value. Product managers must evaluate the benefits, risks, feasibility, and ethical implications before deciding whether AI is the right solution.
