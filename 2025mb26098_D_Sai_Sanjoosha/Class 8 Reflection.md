Building AI Products and Managing Changing Technology

This session provided practical insights into the challenges involved in developing AI-based products, particularly with the rapid evolution of AI development tools and technologies. The discussion around agentic coding and AI-assisted software development was especially interesting because it demonstrated how quickly the product development environment is changing.
One of the important points discussed was the changing performance of AI development tools and models. A tool that performs well during one development iteration may produce inconsistent results during another. This can make product development challenging because teams may depend on external AI providers and their underlying models.
I found the distinction between model-related limitations and provider-side instability particularly useful. A model may have certain limitations in its performance, but fluctuations in results may also be caused by changes in the provider's infrastructure, model versions, or the development environment. Product teams need to monitor these changes rather than assuming that every issue is caused by their own implementation.
The discussion also highlighted the importance of maintaining proper project documentation and managing feedback effectively. The faculty emphasized that teams should maintain their project proposals in the specified repository, document feedback as actionable issues, and take responsibility for resolving those issues. I understood that receiving feedback is only useful when it is converted into improvements and tracked until completion.
Another important learning was the need for flexibility in AI product development. Since the technology is evolving rapidly, product managers should be prepared to reassess their decisions, evaluate alternative tools, and adapt their development approach when required.
My key takeaway: This session helped me understand that AI product management requires continuous learning and adaptability. Product managers must be aware of technological changes, understand external dependencies, monitor product performance, and ensure that the development team remains accountable. In a rapidly changing AI environment, the ability to adapt is as important as having a clear product vision.
