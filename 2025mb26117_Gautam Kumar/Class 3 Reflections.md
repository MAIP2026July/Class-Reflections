### 1. Key Learnings & Takeaways
* **Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) Over Feature Lists:** Customers do not buy AI models, prompts, or neural networks; they hire a product to help them make progress in a specific job or workflow.
* **Products vs Projects:** Projects have a fixed schedule, budget, and end date. A product is a living system that requires continuous adaptation, monitoring, and value capture.
* **Moats in the Age of Commoditized Models:** When every competitor has access to the same foundation models via API, competitive advantage shifts to proprietary data, workflow integration, and speed of customer feedback loops.
* **Transformation vs Optimization:** Optimization makes an existing workflow 10% faster. Transformation rethinks why the workflow existed in the first place.

---

### 2. What Resonated Most With Me
Today's deep dive into **value capture** really clarified where AI products win or lose. Just because an AI feature feels magical does not mean it translates into business value. If you build something that saves an analyst 30 minutes but your API bills cost $20 per query, you are running a charity, not a product.

Professor Vinaya's point that **the target is constantly moving** in AI product management hit home. Unlike traditional software where functional requirements can remain stable for months, user expectations and model capabilities shift rapidly. Therefore, a product plan must be structured around proof gates and learning milestones rather than rigid multi-year roadmaps.

I also appreciated the emphasis on addressing compliance, regulatory guardrails, and inclusion right at the concept phase rather than treating them as an afterthought right before launch.

### 3. Constructive Thoughts & What Could Be Explored Further
* The JTBD framework is fantastic, but applying it to B2B enterprise AI workflows has unique complexities because the buyer (e.g., CFO/CIO) often has a completely different job to be done than the end-user (e.g., staff analyst). A quick 15-minute group exercise mapping dual-persona JTBD would be a great addition to this topic.
