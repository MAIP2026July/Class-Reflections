### 1. Key Learnings & Takeaways
* **Start With the Problem, Never the Tech:** The fastest way to build an expensive failure is to start with a cool model capability and go searching for a problem to attach it to.
* **Reimagining Workflows vs Paving the Cowpath:** Simply bolting an AI chatbot onto an outdated, broken workflow only gives you an automated broken workflow. True product innovation comes from restructuring how work gets done.
* **The Customer Articulation Gap:** Customers know what is frustrating them, but they rarely know how to articulate the optimal technical solution. PMs must observe actual user behavior rather than just transcribing feature requests.
* **The Surveillance Paradox:** To personalize and train effective models, we want rich user data; however, excessive telemetry makes users feel monitored and erodes fundamental trust.

---

### 2. What Resonated Most With Me
Since this was the midpoint of our semester, it was very rewarding to see the threads from earlier sessions connect together. The concept of **paving the cowpath** really stood out. When organizations adopted computers in the 1980s, they initially just digitized physical paper forms without changing the workflow. We are seeing the exact same mistake today with people shoving chatbots into interfaces that should actually be autonomous background jobs or simple rule-based triggers.

The **surveillance paradox** also prompted a lot of reflection. In enterprise productivity tools, telemetry must be designed with data minimization in mind. Transparent logging, clear data retention policies, and showing users *why* an AI recommendation was made are essential for long-term retention.
