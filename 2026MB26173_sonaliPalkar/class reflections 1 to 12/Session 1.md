## **MANAGEMENT OF AI PRODUCTS** 

# **Session 1 – Introduction to Management of AI Products** 

The first session helped me understand that product management is much broader than managing a project or coordinating development activities. A product manager takes ownership of the product and is responsible for connecting a real societal or customer problem with a solution that people find valuable. What stood out to me is the idea of being a creator while also being accountable for the resources and trust placed in us. 

One important learning was the three dimensions of AI in product management. The first is using AI to build the product, including development tools and coding assistants. The second is using AI to improve product-management work, such as preparing and reviewing product requirements and other management artefacts. The third is embedding AI into the product so that customers experience AI as part of the solution. These are distinct areas, and a product manager needs to understand the implications of each. 

I also understood that the course is intended to connect concepts with practical application through a product-building project. Frameworks are not meant to be memorised simply for the sake of it; they should help us analyse a business problem and make better decisions. This is a useful way to approach management tools in general: first understand the problem and the decision required, and then select a framework that supports it. 

My personal takeaway is that product management requires curiosity, ownership and continuous learning. It is important to participate, make notes and reflect on what is useful in my own work rather than only memorising what is discussed in class. I would like to apply this mindset by looking beyond execution and understanding the business problem, the value expected by stakeholders and how technology—including AI—can help create that value. 

Overall, the session gave me a clear foundation: successful AI product management is not only about technology. It is about identifying meaningful problems, making informed decisions and creating products that deliver value.
