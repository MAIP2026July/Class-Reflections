# Class 1 Reflection

## Session 1: Introduction to the Management of AI Products

### Discussion points

AI products are socio-technical systems in which data, models, software,
users, and business processes jointly determine the product's behaviour.

### Types of AI products

1.  **Prediction products** --- Forecasting and classification (for
    example, fraud detection).
2.  **Recommendation systems** --- Use user context and preferences to
    recommend or suggest options. A model ranks options and makes
    recommendations.
3.  **Generative AI products** --- Create new content (for example,
    ChatGPT).

### How AI supports business action

The basic flow discussed was:

**Data → Model → Prediction → Business Action**

AI products use models to solve business problems.

### AI value

> **AI value = Model capability × Business usefulness × User adoption**

The value of an AI product depends not only on the model's capability,
but also on how useful it is to the business and whether users adopt it.

### Reflection

The Product Manager is the decision-maker who makes the product,
including an AI product, valuable.
